**Başlık:** The Doomsday Vault Hidden Inside an Arctic Mountain

**Açıklama:**
```
Inside a mountain on Svalbard, Norway, about 1,300 km from the North Pole, sits the Svalbard Global Seed Vault: a backup of the world's crops. A tunnel leads more than 100 m into the rock to chambers kept at about -18°C, and the surrounding permafrost helps keep the seeds cold even if the power fails. It now holds more than 1.3 million seed samples. In 2015, the war in Syria led to the vault's first withdrawal: ICARDA, cut off from its gene bank near Aleppo, took back its backup seeds and grew them again.

**Başlık:** Russia's Secret Mountain: What Is Inside Yamantau?

**Açıklama:**
```
Mount Yamantau (1,640 m) is the highest peak of the Southern Urals, in Bashkortostan, Russia. At its foot lies Mezhgorye, a closed military town (formerly Beloretsk-15/16). Since the late Soviet era, a large underground complex has reportedly been built there. Asked about it, Russian officials gave changing explanations — a mining site, a store for treasures, a food warehouse, a bunker for leaders — and in 1997 the U.S. House of Representatives passed a resolution asking Russia to explain the project and allow access. Many Western analysts believe it is a command bunker for nuclear war, but its true purpose has never been officially confirmed. Everything about the inside is unverified.

## 3) Star Dust / STENDEC — `video/out/gizem-stardust-en.mp4` (58.4 sn)
**Başlık:** STENDEC: The Plane That Vanished and Came Out of a Glacier

**Açıklama:**
```
On 2 August 1947, Star Dust, a British South American Airways Avro Lancastrian with 11 people aboard, was flying from Buenos Aires to Santiago when it vanished over the Andes. Its last Morse message gave its arrival time in Santiago and ended with the word "STENDEC" — a word that has never been explained. In 1998, Argentine climbers found an engine and wreckage emerging from a glacier on Mount Tupungato, and later expeditions recovered more. Investigators concluded that the crew, flying in cloud into a strong jet-stream headwind that was poorly understood at the time, believed they had already crossed the Andes and began descending too early.

Sources (facts and the quoted "STENDEC" message): Argentine Air Force investigation report (2000); NOVA / PBS, "Vanished!" (2001); Aviation Safety Network, G-AGWH record — https://aviation-safety.net

## 4) Bermuda Triangle / Flight 19 — `video/out/gizem-bermuda-en.mp4` (57.9 sn)
**Başlık:** Flight 19: The Disappearance That Created the Bermuda Triangle

**Açıklama:**
```
On 5 December 1945, Flight 19 — five U.S. Navy TBM Avenger torpedo bombers with 14 men, led by Lt. Charles Taylor — left NAS Fort Lauderdale on a navigation training flight and never returned. Taylor reported that his compasses had failed and believed he was over the Florida Keys; the flight became lost as night fell and fuel ran low. A PBM Mariner flying boat with 13 men sent to search also disappeared; a passing ship reported a burst of flame, and Mariners were known as "flying gas tanks". The Navy's final report listed the cause as unknown. 27 men were lost, and the story became the centrepiece of the Bermuda Triangle legend. But the area is not statistically more dangerous than other heavily travelled stretches of ocean: the U.S. Coast Guard does not recognise it as a special danger zone, and Lloyd's of London does not treat it as more risky.

Sources (facts and the quoted "flying gas tanks"): Naval History and Heritage Command, H-Gram 057-4; National Archives, Prologue blog; Smithsonian Magazine; NOAA Ocean Service, "What is the Bermuda Triangle?" — https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/bermudatri.html
